# Changelog

## 2026-11-25 — Research programme established

- Created the literature baseline, candidate matrix, and reference-verification ledger.
- Proved and formalized the finite-order quadratic background theorem, then rejected it as non-original after identifying Forti's Theorem 1.5.
- Proved and fully formalized quadratic stability on dyadically eventually commuting groups.
- Proved and fully formalized sharp Pexider stability on ultrametric semigroups, then classified it as a corollary of Schwaiger's theorem rather than an original flagship.
- Developed and audited additional cone, non-Archimedean, trigonometric, and semigroup candidates.
- Proved and fully formalized normalized Pexider quadratic stability with sharp constants `ε/2` and `ε`; exact-constant novelty audit is in progress.
- Added reproducible Python experiments, 31 passing tests, pinned Lean/Mathlib, LaTeX drafts, and CI configuration.
- No public repository or journal submission has yet been made.
