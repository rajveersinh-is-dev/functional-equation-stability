# Open questions and limitations

## Open questions

1. Determine optimal constants without zero normalization.
2. Extend the sharp estimates to locally convex cones.
3. Investigate targets that are complete only along the relevant dyadic sequences.
4. Determine whether the two bounds improve under parity, symmetry, or continuity assumptions.
5. Seek external peer review of the exact constant vector and its comparison with earlier explicit Pexider bounds.

## Limitations

- The target is a real Banach space.
- The normalization `f(0)=g(0)=0` is essential for the displayed constants.
- “Quadratic” means a solution of the functional equation, not necessarily a continuous homogeneous form.
- The result gives existence of a nearby exact pair, not uniqueness among arbitrary approximate pairs.
- Literature novelty is supported by the documented audit but still requires external peer review.

## Proof status

There are no known blocking gaps in the proof. The central theorem is Lean-verified, and the sharpness example has been independently checked.
