**Review type:** AI-assisted internal review, not independent expert peer review.

**Candidate:** C1, finite-order quadratic stability.

## Findings

- The identities `x^(k+1)x=x^(k+2)` and `x^(k+1)x⁻¹=x^k` are valid in any group.
- The cyclic reindexing uses only powers of one element, so no commutativity of distinct elements is needed.
- The order-one case is consistent: `D_q(1,1)=-2q(1)`.
- Exact quadratic maps on periodic groups vanish in real normed targets because the cyclic identity gives `2n p(x)=0`.
- The set of finite-order elements need not be a subgroup in a nonabelian group, but the proof does not require it to be one.
