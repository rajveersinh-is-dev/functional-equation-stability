## Counterexample search

- order-one elements;
- cyclic groups of both parities;
- elementary abelian two-groups;
- `S3`, `D4`, and `Q8`;

- infinite-order counterexample `q(x)=x²` on `ℝ`;

No mathematical counterexample was found.

## Blocking novelty finding

The candidate is not original. Forti (2019), Theorem 1.5, gives the real-valued `H/2` bound on any group. Applying it to `ℓ∘q` and using norming functionals yields the candidate's normed-space version. Rätz (2003) and Popa–Rus (2026) are additional close results.

## Severity and resolution

**Critical for the research objective.** The theorem is retained only as verified background. It is not used as the flagship contribution.

## Verdict

Reject as an original theorem; accept as a correct expository and formalization asset.
