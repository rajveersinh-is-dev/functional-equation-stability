# Abstract

We study the two-function Pexider quadratic equation on real normed spaces. If `f(0)=g(0)=0` and the uniform defect is at most `ε`, we prove that a quadratic map `Q` satisfies `‖f-Q‖∞≤ε/2` and `‖g-Q‖∞≤ε`. Both constants are sharp. The proof uses the diagonal identity, a dyadic geometric estimate, and a scaled cross-defect limit. No regularity of `f` or `g` is assumed. The central theorem and sharpness lower bounds are formalized in Lean 4.
