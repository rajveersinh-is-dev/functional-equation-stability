# Cover-letter draft

Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript **“Sharp Normalized Stability of the Pexider Quadratic Equation”** by Rajveersinh Vishal Pardeshi.

The paper establishes sharp simultaneous Hyers–Ulam bounds for the normalized two-function Pexider quadratic equation. Under a uniform defect bound `ε`, the two functions admit a common quadratic approximant with errors `ε/2` and `ε`, respectively. Both constants are optimal, and the approximant is unique among quadratic maps at finite uniform distance. The result substantially improves the closest general bounds located in the literature.

The manuscript includes complete proofs, exact-solution normalization, sharpness examples, and a discussion of the relationship to earlier Pexider stability theorems. The central theorem, exact solution structure, sharpness lower bounds, and a uniqueness corollary are formalized in Lean 4. Reproducible computational checks and the research archive accompany the paper.

We confirm that the manuscript has not been submitted elsewhere. No journal acceptance, external peer review, or institutional endorsement is claimed.

Sincerely,

Rajveersinh Vishal Pardeshi
