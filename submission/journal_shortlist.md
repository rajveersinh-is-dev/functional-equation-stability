# Journal shortlist

**Policy snapshot:** 25 November 2026. Fees and policies can change; recheck the official pages immediately before submission. No impact-factor or acceptance-rate claim is made.

## 1. Aequationes Mathematicae — recommended topical fit

Established specialist journal with explicit emphasis on functional equations. Recent relevant work includes a set-valued Pexiderized quadratic paper (online 2024; volume 99, 2025) and a 2026 paper on sharp Ulam constants.

- **Submission:** SNAPP; LaTeX with `birkjour.cls`; English abstract of at most 25 lines; running title, keywords, and MSC; Mathematical Reviews-style references.
- **Review:** Single-anonymous.
- **Fees:** Subscription route has no APC. Optional Gold OA is listed at £2,290 / $3,190 / €2,590.
- **Archiving:** Green accepted-manuscript archiving after 12 months; personal website posting immediately on acceptance.
- **Official pages:** [Scope](https://link.springer.com/journal/10/aims-and-scope), [guidelines](https://link.springer.com/journal/10/submission-guidelines), [fees/OA](https://link.springer.com/journal/10/how-to-publish-with-us).

## 2. Bulletin of the Australian Mathematical Society — concise fee-free option

General-mathematics society journal that prefers well-motivated, relatively short papers. The present manuscript is approximately five pages before journal formatting.

- **Submission:** OJS; AmS-LaTeX PDF; abstract at most 200 words; ORCID, keywords, and MSC 2020; AI-use disclosure.
- **Review:** Single-anonymous.
- **Fees:** Standard Green route has no publication charge. Optional Gold OA is listed at US$3,655 for 2026.
- **Archiving:** Preprints anytime; accepted manuscript on a non-commercial repository after six months; final version free after five years.
- **Official pages:** [Journal](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/bulletin-of-the-australian-mathematical-society), [submissions](https://journal.austms.org.au/ojs/index.php/Bulletin/about/submissions).

## 3. Publicationes Mathematicae Debrecen — fee-free fallback

Long-running university journal covering pure mathematics.

- **Submission:** Email PDF; correct English; LaTeX preferred and required after acceptance; 4–6-line abstract; MSC and keywords; AI-use disclosure where relevant.
- **Review:** Minimum two anonymous reviewers.
- **Fees:** No submission fee or APC.
- **Archiving:** Older volumes are open; pre-galley versions may be repository-posted, but the published version may not be.
- **Official pages:** [Scope](https://publi.math.unideb.hu/aims-and-scope), [author instructions](https://publi.math.unideb.hu/for-authors).

## 4. Annals of Formalized Mathematics — conditional Lean-centered option

A newer diamond open-access journal for proof-assistant mathematics. A viable option only if the formalization is presented as a central contribution rather than an appendix.

- **Submission:** Deposit on HAL, arXiv, or Zenodo and link an open-source artifact to the main results; human-authored manuscript and AI/funding disclosure.
- **Review:** Single-blind.
- **Fees:** No publication or access charge; CC BY 4.0.
- **Caution:** Mere formalization of a known result is out of scope; indexing and throughput were not verified.
- **Official pages:** [Scope](https://afm.episciences.org/page/aims-and-scope), [instructions](https://afm.episciences.org/page/instructions-for-authors), [policies](https://afm.episciences.org/page/publishing-policies).

## 5. Journal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications — wider reach, higher bar

Suitable if the sharp constants are judged to have significance beyond the functional-equations community.

- **Submission:** Elsevier portal; factual abstract at most 250 words; 1–7 keywords and declarations.
- **Review:** Single-anonymized.
- **Fees:** Subscription route has no publication fee; optional Gold OA is listed at US$3,380 excluding taxes.
- **Official pages:** [Author guide](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-mathematical-analysis-and-applications/publish/guide-for-authors), [OA options](https://www.elsevier.com/journals/journal-of-mathematical-analysis-and-applications/0022-247X/open-access-options).

## Not preferred: Journal of Inequalities and Applications

Topically plausible, but the fully open-access model has a mandatory APC listed at £1,490 / $2,290 / €1,990, subject to waivers. This conflicts with the preference for no mandatory author fees. [Official guidelines](https://link.springer.com/journal/13660/submission-guidelines).
